SciCon Geophysics

Senior scientific consulting.

We bring together three senior specialists to address complex, non-standard geophysical questions with rigorous methods and pragmatic advice.

Every engagement starts with the technical decision that needs to be supported. We make assumptions visible, choose methods for the question at hand, and communicate findings together with their uncertainty and limitations.

01

Experience brought into SciCon

Our team brings experience from seismic monitoring applications in volcanic environments in East Africa and the western Pacific region.

This experience was gained by team members before the founding of SciCon Geophysics.

Services

Seismic monitoring, analysis, and assessment.

From monitoring concepts and network performance to specialised data interpretation and independent assessments, our work is scoped to the evidence available and the decision you need to make.

01

Seismic Monitoring & Network Design

Planning and assessment of seismic monitoring concepts and network configurations.

  • Seismic and seismological monitoring concepts
  • Network analysis, configuration, and optimisation
  • Performance, detection capability, and data quality
  • Monitoring strategies for subsurface projects
02

Advanced Seismic Data Analysis

Processing and interpretation of seismic data using methods appropriate to the available observations.

  • Event detection, localisation, and source characterisation
  • Signal processing, array, and network analysis
  • Numerical modelling
  • Data-driven and machine-learning approaches
03

DAS & Borehole Seismology

Analysis of DAS, borehole, and surface seismic data, including existing VSP data.

Scope note: We provide VSP data processing, analysis, interpretation, and optional survey-design support. SciCon does not offer VSP field acquisition.

  • DAS applications for seismic monitoring
  • DAS and borehole seismic data analysis
  • VSP data processing and interpretation
  • Integration of borehole and surface observations
04

Independent Assessments & Special Studies

Independent reviews and studies for specific technical and regulatory questions.

  • Technical reviews and expert assessments
  • Induced seismicity
  • Effects of wind farms on seismic measurements
  • Custom scientific and geophysical studies

How we work

Our consulting process.

The exact scope is agreed individually. The process remains transparent, proportionate, and grounded in the quality of the available evidence.

  1. 01

    Define the question

    Understand the project context, available data, constraints, and decision to be supported.

  2. 02

    Assess the evidence

    Review data quality, network performance, assumptions, and applicable methods.

  3. 03

    Analyse & interpret

    Apply appropriate signal processing, modelling, statistical, or machine-learning techniques.

  4. 04

    Present the findings

    Communicate findings, uncertainty, and actionable recommendations in a useful form.
