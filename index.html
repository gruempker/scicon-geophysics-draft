SciCon Geophysics

Senior scientific consulting.

We bring together three senior specialists to address complex, non-standard geophysical questions with rigorous methods and pragmatic advice.

Every engagement starts with the technical decision that needs to be supported. We make assumptions visible, choose methods for the question at hand, and communicate findings together with their uncertainty and limitations.

01

Experience

Our team combines decades of experience in seismic monitoring and advanced data analysis, with a particular focus on geothermal applications and induced seismicity in the Upper Rhine Graben. This includes pioneering applications of seismic array methods for monitoring induced earthquakes in the Landau and Insheim areas. Further applications include seismic monitoring in volcanic environments in East Africa and the western Pacific.

Services

Seismic monitoring, analysis, and assessment.

From monitoring concepts and network performance to specialised data interpretation and independent assessments, our work is scoped to the evidence available and the decision you need to make.

Research & method development

Research on emerging DAS methods.

Alongside our established consulting services, members of the SciCon team contribute to the development of new methodologies for Distributed Acoustic Sensing.

Patent application: Members of the SciCon team are named as inventors on European patent application EP25186377.5, filed by Goethe University Frankfurt. The application relates to the research discussed in the publications below.

Research stage

Passive localisation of fibre-optic cables

Seismology-based approaches for determining the geometry of near-surface fibre-optic cables from DAS observations, using ambient-noise correlations or plane-wave arrivals.

Rümpker, G., Limberger, F. & Komeazi, A. (2026), Scientific Reports, 16, 16843.

Read the publication ↗

Research stage

Wavefield-based event localisation

Wavefield-based imaging for locating seismic events in heterogeneous media using borehole DAS and numerical modelling of location-dependent wavefield patterns.

Komeazi, A., Limberger, F. & Rümpker, G. (2026), The Leading Edge.

Read the publication ↗

Current scope: These approaches are at the research and validation stage and are not offered as production-ready services. Any application would begin with a feasibility assessment and validation against site-specific data.

How we work

Our consulting process.

The exact scope is agreed individually. The process remains transparent, proportionate, and grounded in the quality of the available evidence.

  1. 01

    Define the question

    Understand the project context, available data, constraints, and decision to be supported.

  2. 02

    Assess the evidence

    Review data quality, network performance, assumptions, and applicable methods.

  3. 03

    Analyse & interpret

    Apply appropriate signal processing, modelling, statistical, or machine-learning techniques.

  4. 04

    Present the findings

    Communicate findings, uncertainty, and actionable recommendations in a useful form.
